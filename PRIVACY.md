# Privacy Policy

ORBIT, ORBIT Academy, ORBIT Code, KASTT accounts and the ORBIT Discord application. Created by Outkastt.

Last updated 17 November 2026

## The short version

- The apps run on your computer. Your files, notes, backups and code never leave it.
- An account is optional. If you sign in, we keep a small profile so your progress follows you between apps and computers.
- We do not sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.
- You can delete your account and everything synced to it at any time.

## What the apps keep on your computer

Settings, notes, backup jobs, learning progress, XP and coding stats are saved in your Windows user folder (for example %APPDATA%\ORBIT Suite). If you sign in, the apps store a sign in token there, encrypted with Windows’ own protection for your user account. Uninstalling an app does not delete your notes or backups.

## What we keep if you sign in

When you sign in with Google, Discord, GitHub or Cfx.re we receive and store:

- your account ID with that provider, so we can recognise you next time
- your display name and profile picture link from that provider
- a profile picture you upload yourself, if you choose to
- your synced progress: XP and how it was earned, badges, learning and lab progress, and coding stats such as hours and lines written (counts only, never the code itself)
- a list of the computers you have linked, with a short label, when each was linked and when it was last seen
- sign in sessions, stored only as one way hashes of the token

We only read your basic public profile. We do not ask providers for your email address, contacts, servers, friends or files. For Cfx.re, the forum issues a temporary key that we use once to read your username and picture, then revoke straight away.

## How we use it

Only to run your account: signing you in, showing your name and picture in the apps and on the Account page, and syncing progress between your apps and computers. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (contract) and, for anything optional such as your uploaded picture, your choice to provide it.

## Discord activity

If Discord is running, the apps send your current activity (such as the topic you are learning, the lab you are in, or the language and file or project name you are editing) directly to the Discord client on your computer, through the ORBIT Discord application. Discord then shows it on your profile to people who can see your activity. This does not pass through our servers. In ORBIT Code you can hide file and project names, and you can turn activity off completely in each app’s settings. Discord handles that data under its own privacy policy.

## Other connections the apps make

- Update checks to fivemkastt.com, which return the latest version number. Your web address (IP) is seen by our host, Cloudflare, as with any website visit.
- Downloading installers from GitHub, and installing apps through Windows Package Manager, when you choose to.
- Opening links in your web browser when you click them.

There is no analytics, crash reporting or advertising tracking in the apps.

## Cookies

The website sets one cookie when you sign in, to keep you signed in for up to 30 days. It is essential for the account to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies for your account.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your data. It is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the website and database for us. The sign in provider you choose (Google, Discord, GitHub or Cfx.re) knows you used it to sign in to KASTT. Purchases in the KASTT script store are handled by Tebex under [their privacy policy](https://www.tebex.io/privacy).

## How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Website sessions expire after 30 days, and you can unlink any computer at any time. When you delete your account, your profile, synced progress, picture, linked sign ins and sessions are deleted from our database.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Most of this you can do yourself on the [Account page](https://fivemkastt.com/account/). For anything else, contact us below. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Children

The apps are for learning and anyone can use them without an account. Accounts depend on your sign in provider’s own age rules (for example, Discord requires users to be at least 13). We do not knowingly collect more than described here from anyone.

## Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
